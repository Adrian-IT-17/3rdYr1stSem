## Q1

**Question:** Which of the following is NOT a type of feasibility study?

**Type:** single

**Options:**

- A. Technical feasibility
- B. Economic feasibility
- C. Operational feasibility
- D. Logical feasibility

**Answer:** D

**Explanation:** Technical, economic, and operational feasibility are recognized areas used to evaluate whether a proposed system is practical. Logical feasibility is not one of the standard feasibility categories listed here.

## Q2

**Question:** What is the primary purpose of a business case in system planning?

**Type:** single

**Options:**

- A. To define the system architecture
- B. To create a user interface
- C. To assign project roles
- D. To justify the need for a project

**Answer:** D

**Explanation:** A business case explains why a proposed project should be undertaken by establishing its need and expected value.

## Q3

**Question:** What is the first phase of the SDLC Waterfall Model?

**Type:** single

**Options:**

- A. Systems analysis
- B. Systems design
- C. Systems planning
- D. Systems implementation

**Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Systems planning comes first because the project and its overall direction must be established before detailed analysis, design, and implementation begin.

## Q4

**Question:** What tool is used to identify causes and effects in problem analysis?

**Type:** single

**Options:**

- A. Flowchart
- B. SWOT analysis
- C. Fishbone diagram
- D. Gantt chart

**Answer:** C

**Explanation:** A fishbone diagram, also called an Ishikawa or cause-and-effect diagram, organizes possible causes of a problem so they can be investigated.

## Q5

**Type:** single

**Options:**

- A. Government regulations
- B. Strategic plan
- C. Customer demands
- D. Economic conditions

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** A strategic plan originates within the organization and influences its priorities and systems projects. Government regulations, customer demands, and economic conditions are external factors.

## Q6

**Question:** What is the main disadvantage of the SDLC Waterfall Model?

**Type:** single

**Options:**

- A. It is too flexible
- B. It lacks structure
- C. It does not emphasize interactivity among phases
- D. It is too fast-paced

**Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Waterfall follows a structured sequence of phases, so its major limitation is the relatively limited interaction and iteration between phases compared with more adaptive approaches.

## Q11

**Question:** Which planning statement should be logged as an assumption that requires validation?

**Type:** single

**Options:**

- A. The system must follow the institution's approved retention policy
- B. The launch must occur before the enrollment period
- C. Most intended users have reliable access to a compatible smartphone
- D. The pilot budget cannot exceed PHP 120,000

**Answer:** C

**Explanation:** An assumption is something believed to be true but not yet confirmed. Smartphone access must therefore be validated with evidence rather than treated as an established requirement or constraint.
