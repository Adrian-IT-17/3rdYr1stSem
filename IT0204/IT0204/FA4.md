## Q1
**Question:** RA 8792 primarily regulates:  
**Type:** single  
**Options:**
- Patent disputes
- Electronic transactions and digital signatures
- Land ownership
- Banking interest rates  
**Answer:** Electronic transactions and digital signatures  
**Explanation:** RA 8792, the Electronic Commerce Act, gives legal recognition to electronic documents, data messages, and electronic signatures.

## Q2
**Question:** Which type of data requires stricter protection under RA 10173?  
**Type:** single  
**Options:**
- Publicly posted contact details
- Sensitive personal information
- Employee ID number
- Website cookies  
**Answer:** Sensitive personal information  
**Explanation:** RA 10173 gives sensitive personal information stricter safeguards because its misuse or exposure can create greater risks to a person.

## Q3
**Question:** What penalty applies for improper disposal of sensitive personal information under RA 10173?  
**Type:** single  
**Options:**
- Warning from NPC only
- ₱50K fine only
- 1–3 years imprisonment and ₱100K–₱1M fine
- 12 years imprisonment  
**Answer:** 1–3 years imprisonment and ₱100K–₱1M fine  
**Explanation:** Section 27(b) of RA 10173 sets this penalty for improper disposal of sensitive personal information.

## Q17

**Type:** single  
**Options:**
