## Q1
**Question:** Which breach taught the industry about risks in credit-reporting infrastructures?  
**Type:** single  
**Options:**
- British Airways
- Yahoo
- Equifax
- Marriott  
**Answer:** Equifax  
**Explanation:** The breach exposed sensitive information held by a major credit-reporting agency, showing the risk of concentrating personal and financial data in that infrastructure.

## Q2
**Question:** Which IRP phase ensures that chain of custody is preserved for evidence during forensic investigations?  
**Type:** single  
**Options:**
- Identification
- Recovery
- Eradication
- Preparation  
**Answer:** Identification  
**Explanation:** During identification, responders recognize and investigate the incident. They must preserve evidence and document its handling so it remains reliable for forensic or legal review.

## Q3

**Type:** single  
**Options:**
- True
- False  
**Answer:** True  
**Explanation:** Yahoo disclosed major breaches years after the affected activity occurred. Such delays damaged public trust and drew criticism.

## Q4
**Question:** Which breach forced regulators to stress “data minimization” principles?  
**Type:** single  
**Options:**
- British Airways
- Yahoo
- Equifax
- Marriott  
**Answer:** Marriott  
**Explanation:** The Marriott/Starwood incident exposed extensive guest information retained in a reservation system. It is associated with regulatory emphasis on limiting and properly managing retained personal data.

## Q5
**Question:** A company creates an incident logbook documenting actions during a breach. This practice belongs to which IRP phase?  
**Type:** single  
**Options:**
- Lessons Learned
- Preparation
- Recovery
- Identification and Containment  
**Answer:** Identification and Containment  
**Explanation:** Recording actions, timestamps, and decisions during the response supports incident tracking and evidence preservation.

## Q6
**Question:** Which breaches were notable for delayed notifications?  
**Type:** multiple  
**Options:**
- Marriott
- British Airways
- Yahoo
- Equifax  
**Answer:** Marriott, Yahoo, and Equifax  
**Explanation:** Yahoo disclosed older breaches years later, Marriott disclosed the Starwood breach after a long period of unauthorized access, and Equifax disclosed its breach weeks after discovering it. British Airways notified affected parties comparatively promptly.

## Q8
**Question:** If an organization contains a breach but does not conduct forensic analysis, the IRP is still complete.  
**Type:** single  
**Options:**
- False
- True  
**Answer:** False  
**Explanation:** Containment alone may not establish what happened, what data was affected, or whether the threat remains. Investigation and analysis are important parts of incident response.

## Q9

**Type:** single  
**Options:**
- Informing the media
- Backing up data
- Repairing systems
- Investigating and enforcing compliance  
**Answer:** Investigating and enforcing compliance  
**Explanation:** A Data Protection Authority oversees data-protection compliance and may investigate breaches or enforce applicable rules.

## Q10
**Question:** In breach notifications under the Philippine DPA 2012, which information must be provided?  
**Type:** multiple  
**Options:**
- Organization’s market share
- Nature of the breach
- Number of individuals affected
- Measures taken by the organization  
**Answer:** Nature of the breach; Measures taken by the organization  
**Explanation:** Philippine breach-notification rules require a description of the breach and the measures taken to address it. The notification also describes the personal data possibly involved. The number of individuals affected may be requested in reporting, but it is not one of the minimum details stated in the rule.

## Q25
**Question:** In HIPAA, who must be notified if over 500 individuals’ PHI is exposed?  
**Type:** single  
**Options:**
- Only the IT department
- Only regulators
- Patients, HHS, and media
- Only patients  
**Answer:** Patients, HHS, and media  
**Explanation:** For a breach affecting more than 500 people, HIPAA requires notice to affected individuals and HHS. Media notice is also required for breaches affecting more than 500 residents of a state or jurisdiction.

## Q27
**Question:** A Philippine hospital loses unencrypted laptops containing patient records. Which step must follow immediately after containment?  
**Type:** single  
**Options:**
- Purchase new hardware
- Notify staff only
- Erase all logs
- Notify NPC and affected patients  
**Answer:** Notify NPC and affected patients

## Q28
**Question:** A company failing to notify DPAs within 72 hours under GDPR may face heavy fines.  
**Type:** single  
**Options:**
- False
- True  
**Answer:** True  
**Explanation:** GDPR generally requires notification to the supervisory authority within 72 hours of awareness when the breach is likely to pose a risk to people’s rights and freedoms. Late notice may contribute to enforcement or penalties.

## Q29
**Question:** What is the main difference between HIPAA and GDPR breach notifications?  
**Type:** single  
**Options:**
- GDPR has no timeline requirement
- HIPAA applies globally
- GDPR only applies to healthcare
- HIPAA applies to PHI, GDPR covers all personal data  
**Answer:** HIPAA applies to PHI, GDPR covers all personal data  
**Explanation:** HIPAA applies to protected health information handled by covered entities and business associates. GDPR applies more broadly to personal data within its scope.

## Q30
**Question:** Which body oversees breach notifications under GDPR?  
**Type:** single  
**Options:**
- Department of Justice
- Data Protection Authorities (DPAs)
- U.S. Federal Trade Commission
- Philippine NPC  
**Answer:** Data Protection Authorities (DPAs)  
**Explanation:** GDPR breach notifications go to the competent supervisory authority, commonly called a Data Protection Authority.
