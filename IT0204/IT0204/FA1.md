# IT0204 Formative Assessment 1

## Q1
**Question:** Which are benefits of international harmonization of cybersecurity laws?
**Type:** multi
**Options:**
- Consistent data protection standards
- Increased local isolation
- Facilitation of trade
- Easier compliance for global businesses
**Answer:** Consistent data protection standards; Facilitation of trade; Easier compliance for global businesses
**Explanation:** International harmonization helps create consistent data protection standards, facilitates international trade, and makes compliance easier for businesses operating across multiple countries. Increased local isolation is not a benefit of harmonization.

## Q2
**Question:** Which is a key risk of weak jurisdictional enforcement in data privacy?
**Type:** single
**Options:**
- Easier compliance
- Cross-border misuse of data
- Increased software patents
- Lower cybercrime rates
**Answer:** Cross-border misuse of data
**Explanation:** Weak jurisdictional enforcement can make it difficult to control how personal data is handled across national borders, increasing the risk of cross-border misuse of data.

## Q3
**Question:** What does encryption ensure?
**Type:** single
**Options:**
- Backup of files
- Confidentiality and integrity of data
- Permanent deletion of data
- Unauthorized sharing
**Answer:** Confidentiality and integrity of data
**Explanation:** Encryption protects data from unauthorized access and helps maintain its confidentiality and integrity.

## Q4
**Question:** The Data Privacy Act of 2012 was enacted in:
**Type:** single
**Options:**
- Philippines
- Japan
- Canada
- United States
**Answer:** Philippines
**Explanation:** The Data Privacy Act of 2012, or Republic Act No. 10173, is a Philippine law that protects personal information and regulates its processing.

## Q5
**Question:** A company processes personal data of EU citizens without GDPR compliance. This is lawful.
**Type:** single
**Options:**
- True
- False
**Answer:** False
**Explanation:** Organizations subject to the GDPR must comply with its requirements when processing covered personal data. Processing such data without required GDPR compliance is not lawful.

## Q6

**Type:** single
**Options:**
- Improved traffic management
- Facilitating trade and ensuring consistent protection
- Faster elections
- Cheaper food supply
**Answer:** Facilitating trade and ensuring consistent protection
**Explanation:** Harmonized cybersecurity laws can facilitate international trade while providing more consistent standards of protection across different jurisdictions.

## Q7
**Question:** Which is NOT an example of a cybersecurity incident?
**Type:** single
**Options:**
- Data theft
- Unauthorized access
- Ransomware attack
- System software update
**Answer:** System software update
**Explanation:** A normal system software update is a legitimate maintenance activity, while data theft, unauthorized access, and ransomware attacks are cybersecurity incidents.

- True

## Q9

## Q13
**Question:** A hospital encrypts patient data but lets all employees access it without logging. This complies with confidentiality.
**Type:** single
**Options:**
- False
- True
**Answer:** False
**Explanation:** Encryption alone does not ensure proper confidentiality. Access should also be restricted to authorized personnel and appropriately monitored or logged.

## Q14
**Question:** A retailer refuses to correct inaccurate customer data when requested. This complies with privacy rights.
**Type:** single
**Options:**
- False
- True
**Answer:** False
**Explanation:** Privacy laws commonly provide individuals with rights concerning the correction or rectification of inaccurate personal information. Refusing a valid correction request would therefore not comply with those rights.

## Q15
**Question:** What is the primary goal of encryption in privacy law compliance?
**Type:** single
**Options:**
- To advertise security
- To prevent unauthorized access to sensitive data

**Answer:** To prevent unauthorized access to sensitive data

## Q16

## Q19
**Question:** Which are examples of sector-specific regulations?
**Type:** multi
**Options:**
- HIPAA
- Kyoto Protocol
- CFAA
- GLBA
**Answer:** HIPAA; GLBA
**Explanation:** HIPAA contains privacy and security requirements for protected health information in the healthcare sector, while GLBA includes privacy and safeguarding requirements for financial institutions. The Kyoto Protocol concerns climate change, while the CFAA primarily addresses computer-related crimes.

## Q20
**Question:** What is the purpose of pseudonymization?
**Type:** single
**Options:**
- Encrypting files permanently
- Reducing re-identification risk by replacing identifiers
- Creating anonymous emails
- Hiding passwords in code
**Answer:** Reducing re-identification risk by replacing identifiers
**Explanation:** Pseudonymization replaces or separates identifying information so that personal data cannot be directly attributed to a specific person without additional information, reducing re-identification risk.

## Q22
**Question:** Which are elements of the CIA Triad?
**Type:** multi
**Options:**
- Integrity
- Confidentiality

- Availability

## Q23
**Question:** A Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA) is required under GDPR when:
**Type:** single
**Options:**
- Organizations collect basic customer names
- Marketing surveys are conducted
- Data is stored in hard drives

**Answer:** Processing poses high risk to individuals’ rights
**Explanation:** Under the GDPR, a DPIA is required when a type of processing is likely to result in a high risk to the rights and freedoms of individuals.

## Q26

- False
- True
