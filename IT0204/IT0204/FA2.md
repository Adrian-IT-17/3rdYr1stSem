## Q1
**Question:** Which data subject rights exist under GDPR but not explicitly under CCPA?  
**Type:** single  
**Options:**
- Right to rectification
- Right to be forgotten
- Right to opt out of data sale
- Right to restriction of processing  
**Answer:** Right to restriction of processing  
**Explanation:** GDPR explicitly provides the right to restrict processing under certain conditions. CCPA provides rights to correct and delete information and to opt out of its sale or sharing.

## Q2
**Question:** Which law has extraterritorial scope, applying to organizations outside its jurisdiction?  
**Type:** single  
**Options:**
- HIPAA
- CCPA
- GDPR
- DPA 2012  
**Answer:** GDPR  
**Explanation:** GDPR can apply to organizations outside the European Union when they offer goods or services to people in the EU or monitor their behavior there.

## Q3
**Question:** Under GDPR, consent must be freely given, specific, and informed.  
**Type:** single  
**Options:**
- True
- False  
**Answer:** True  
**Explanation:** Valid GDPR consent must be freely given, specific, informed, and unambiguous and must involve a clear affirmative action.

## Q4
**Question:** Which is an adequacy mechanism under GDPR?  
**Type:** single  
**Options:**
- Adequacy Decision by EU Commission
- U.S. Federal Courts
- Data Minimization
- WTO Approval  
**Answer:** Adequacy Decision by EU Commission  
**Explanation:** The European Commission may decide that another country, territory, or international organization provides an adequate level of data protection.

## Q5
**Question:** Which law applies to California companies with $25M+ revenue or 50K+ consumer data?  
**Type:** single  
**Options:**
- GDPR
- DPA 2012
- HIPAA
- CCPA  
**Answer:** CCPA  
**Explanation:** These were applicability thresholds associated with the CCPA. The current thresholds have since been updated, but CCPA is still the intended answer.

## Q6
**Question:** Which of the following are GDPR data subject rights?  
**Type:** multiple  
**Options:**
- Right to be informed
- Right to restrict processing
- Right to sell data for profit
- Right to erasure  
**Answer:** Right to be informed; Right to restrict processing; Right to erasure  
**Explanation:** GDPR gives individuals rights concerning information, restriction of processing, and erasure. It does not establish a right to sell personal data for profit.

## Q7
**Question:** Which Philippine DPA violation involves intentionally sharing data with a competitor?  
**Type:** single  
**Options:**
- Unauthorized Processing
- Negligence in Access
- Concealment
- Malicious Disclosure  
**Answer:** Malicious Disclosure  
**Explanation:** Section 31 of RA 10173 covers the disclosure of personal information with malice or in bad faith.

## Q8
**Question:** Under HIPAA, which type of data is specially protected?  
**Type:** single  
**Options:**
- Protected Health Information (PHI)
- Student Grades
- Tax Records
- IP Addresses  
**Answer:** Protected Health Information (PHI)  
**Explanation:** HIPAA protects individually identifiable health information held or transmitted by covered entities and their business associates.

## Q9
**Question:** A healthcare provider uses strong encryption and access control to protect patient records. This aligns with HIPAA safeguards.  
**Type:** single  
**Options:**
- False
- True  
**Answer:** True  
**Explanation:** Encryption and access controls help protect electronic PHI and align with HIPAA’s technical safeguard requirements.

## Q10
**Question:** A retailer publishes anonymized sales data with no identifiers. This generally complies with privacy laws.  
**Type:** single  
**Options:**
- True
- False  
**Answer:** True  
**Explanation:** Properly anonymized information that cannot reasonably identify individuals generally falls outside personal data protections.

## Q11
**Question:** Which Philippine DPA violation occurs when ex-employees leak data in bad faith?  
**Type:** single  
**Options:**
- Malicious Disclosure
- Concealment
- Improper Disposal
- Unauthorized Purpose  
**Answer:** Malicious Disclosure  
**Explanation:** Deliberately leaking personal information in bad faith falls under malicious disclosure in Section 31 of RA 10173.

## Q12
**Question:** Which Philippine violation involves throwing printed patient records in a public trash bin?  
**Type:** single  
**Options:**
- Concealment of Breach
- Negligence in Access
- Unauthorized Purpose
- Improper Disposal  
**Answer:** Improper Disposal  
**Explanation:** Throwing sensitive records into a publicly accessible trash bin is improper disposal because unauthorized people may access the information.

## Q13
**Question:** A U.S. hospital fails to provide patients with a copy of their medical records. This violates HIPAA.  
**Type:** single  
**Options:**
- False
- True  
**Answer:** True  
**Explanation:** HIPAA generally gives patients the right to inspect and obtain copies of their medical and billing records.

## Q14
**Question:** Which law sets penalties up to €20M or 4% turnover?  
**Type:** single  
**Options:**
- GDPR
- CCPA
- DPA 2012
- HIPAA  
**Answer:** GDPR  
**Explanation:** Serious GDPR violations may result in fines of up to €20 million or 4% of total worldwide annual turnover, whichever is higher.

## Q15
**Question:** A healthcare clinic locks filing cabinets with patient data. This is a HIPAA safeguard.  
**Type:** single  
**Options:**
- False
- True  
**Answer:** True  
**Explanation:** Locking filing cabinets is a physical safeguard that helps prevent unauthorized access to patient information.

## Q16
**Question:** Which CCPA right allows consumers to stop companies from selling their data?  
**Type:** single  
**Options:**
- Right to Rectification
- Right to Opt Out
- Right to Access
- Right to Delete  
**Answer:** Right to Opt Out  
**Explanation:** CCPA allows consumers to direct businesses to stop selling or sharing their personal information.

## Q17
**Question:** A business processing fewer than 50,000 California users’ data is exempt from CCPA.  
**Type:** single  
**Options:**
- False
- True  
**Answer:** False  
**Explanation:** Processing fewer than 50,000 users’ data does not automatically exempt a business. It may still fall under CCPA because of its revenue or income from selling or sharing personal information.

## Q18
**Question:** Which Philippine DPA violation combines two or more offenses?  
**Type:** single  
**Options:**
- Negligence in Access
- Combination of Acts
- Concealment
- Malicious Disclosure  
**Answer:** Combination of Acts  
**Explanation:** Section 33 of RA 10173 applies when a person commits a combination or series of offenses covered by Sections 25 to 32.

## Q19
**Question:** A company must disclose the purpose for which it collects data under CCPA.  
**Type:** single  
**Options:**
- True
- False  
**Answer:** True  
**Explanation:** CCPA requires businesses to tell consumers, at or before collection, what personal information they collect and the purposes for collecting or using it.

## Q20
**Question:** Which is a key consumer right under GDPR?  
**Type:** single  
**Options:**
- Right to unlimited data use
- Right to ownership of companies
- Right to profit
- Right to data portability  
**Answer:** Right to data portability  
**Explanation:** GDPR allows individuals to receive certain personal data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format.

## Q21
**Question:** Which right under GDPR lets individuals transfer data between providers?  
**Type:** single  
**Options:**
- Right to Object
- Right to Delete
- Right to Portability
- Right to Rectification  
**Answer:** Right to Portability  
**Explanation:** The right to data portability allows individuals to receive and transfer certain personal data from one controller to another.

## Q22
**Question:** Which of the following is NOT a key GDPR principle?  
**Type:** single  
**Options:**
- Profit Maximization
- Accuracy
- Accountability
- Storage Limitation  
**Answer:** Profit Maximization  
**Explanation:** Accuracy, accountability, and storage limitation are GDPR principles. Profit maximization is a business objective rather than a data protection principle.

## Q23
**Question:** Which GDPR right allows individuals to stop processing under certain conditions?  
**Type:** single  
**Options:**
- Right to Restrict Processing
- Portability
- Right to be Forgotten
- Right to Rectification  
**Answer:** Right to Restrict Processing  
**Explanation:** This right allows individuals to limit how an organization processes their personal data when specified conditions apply.

## Q24
**Question:** A Philippine university posts student data publicly without consent. This violates DPA 2012.  
**Type:** single  
**Options:**
- True
- False  
**Answer:** True  
**Explanation:** Publicly disclosing student personal information without consent or another lawful basis may violate the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012.

## Q25
**Question:** A company hides a cyber breach from customers to avoid reputational damage. This complies with GDPR.  
**Type:** single  
**Options:**
- False
- True  
**Answer:** False  
**Explanation:** GDPR may require notification to the supervisory authority and affected individuals when a breach reaches the applicable risk thresholds. Concealing it to avoid reputational harm does not comply.

## Q26
**Question:** Which Philippine law introduced criminal penalties for data privacy violations?  
**Type:** single  
**Options:**
- Freedom of Information Act
- Data Privacy Act of 2012
- E-Commerce Act
- Consumer Act of 1992  
**Answer:** Data Privacy Act of 2012  
**Explanation:** RA 10173 establishes criminal penalties for offenses such as unauthorized processing, improper disposal, breach concealment, and malicious disclosure.

## Q27

**Type:** single  
**Options:**
- Minimization
- Accountability
- Transparency
- Portability  
**Answer:** Accountability  
**Explanation:** Accountability requires organizations to comply with GDPR principles and maintain evidence showing how they comply.

**Type:** single  
**Options:**
- False
- True  
**Answer:** True

## Q29
**Question:** Which are obligations under HIPAA?  
**Type:** multiple  
**Options:**
- Implement safeguards
- Protect PHI
- Sell patient data freely
- Provide breach notification  
**Answer:** Implement safeguards; Protect PHI; Provide breach notification  
**Explanation:** HIPAA requires covered organizations to protect PHI, apply appropriate safeguards, and provide required breach notifications. It does not permit patient data to be sold freely.

## Q30
**Question:** Which of the following does NOT fall under GDPR scope?  
**Type:** single  
**Options:**
- Local clubs with no personal data
- Non-EU businesses processing EU data
- Multinational firms handling EU data
- EU businesses  
**Answer:** Local clubs with no personal data  
**Explanation:** GDPR regulates the processing of personal data. A club that does not process any personal data does not fall within its material scope.
