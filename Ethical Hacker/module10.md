## Q1
**Question:** Which two items are programming logic constructs? (Choose two.)
**Type:** multi
**Options:**
- Boolean operators
- Conditionals
- Arrays
- Dictionaries
- Libraries

## Q4

**Type:** single
**Options:**

## Q5

**Type:** single
